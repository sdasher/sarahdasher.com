Customized interactive problem sets

This is the core of how I work. No more time wasted on flashcards, apps, and books that repeat questions you already know. Books and apps sort practice by broad subject. Your problem sets are built around the specific mistakes where you're leaving points on the table.

  1. Meet with me once

    In one session, we work through problems together so I can see exactly where your reasoning goes off track. Score reports can't show that. The real errors are much more specific than the categories they use.

  2. Get your custom sets

    I generate interactive problem sets that target those growth areas, at the difficulty level you actually need.

  3. Keep growing

    Work through them on your own schedule. When you're ready, I'll build the next set around whatever still needs work.

A report might say "algebra." The actual problem is often one step, like dropping a negative sign when distributing. That step is what the problem set targets, in rising difficulty, until it stops happening.
