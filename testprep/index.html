How it works

Flashcards, apps and prep books repeat questions you already know, sorted by broad subject. Real errors are narrower than that. Every set I write targets yours.

    A custom interactive set aimed at exactly those errors.

    Additional 50-question sets are available for a fee, until you're reliably getting them right.

  5. Move to the next area

    Once one area is solid, we repeat the process on the next.
